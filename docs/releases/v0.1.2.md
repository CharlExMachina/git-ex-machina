Buttons now show when you hover and press them, and copying your sign-in code says it worked.

## Changed

- **Buttons respond to the pointer**: the shaded buttons (the main action, the secondary ones and the destructive ones) didn't change when you hovered or pressed them. They now light up under the pointer and darken while held down.
- **"Code copied"**: when you copy the one-time code while signing in to GitHub, a toast in the window's lower right corner confirms it, then closes on its own.
