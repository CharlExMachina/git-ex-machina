git-ex-machina now shows its own icon in KDE Plasma's launcher and taskbar.

## Fixed

- **The app's icon under KDE Plasma**: with an icon theme that has a `git` icon (Tela, Papirus and others), Plasma showed that icon instead of git-ex-machina's. The app now names its window, desktop entry and icon `com.charlexmachina.GitExMachina`, which no theme shortens to one of its own icons.

## If you installed 0.1.0

Updating replaces the AppImage, but the menu entry your desktop made when you installed it keeps the old icon name. To see the new icon, remove that entry and let your AppImage tool add it again, or point its `Icon=` line at the icon it installed.
